3.2 Services overview
Intro: SwiftMed is a full-scope urgent care, not a limited retail clinic. We treat the everyday illnesses and injuries that can’t wait, with the tools to diagnose them on the spot.
Illness care: Colds, flu, COVID-19, strep throat, sinus and ear infections, urinary tract infections, stomach bugs, rashes, allergies and more.
Injury care: Cuts and lacerations (stitches), sprains and strains, suspected fractures, splinting, burns, wound care, abscess drainage.
Diagnostics: On-site digital X-ray; rapid tests for strep, flu, COVID-19 and urinalysis; blood counts and basic metabolic panels; point-of-care ultrasound.
Preventive and routine: Physical exams, sports and school physicals, immunizations, travel health.
IV hydration and infusions: Physician-supervised. See Section 3.6.
Chronic condition flare-ups: Same-day stabilization. See Section 3.7.
Telehealth: See a provider by video from home or work. Included with membership.
Occupational health: Pre-employment physicals, drug and alcohol testing, DOT exams, workers’ compensation care.
Coming later: Behavioral health screening and referral navigation (anxiety and depression), planned for a later phase.
Urgent care or ER? Short two-column guide. Urgent care: fevers, sprains, minor cuts, UTIs, ear pain, mild asthma flare, rashes. ER (call 911): chest pain, severe shortness of breath, stroke signs, major trauma, uncontrolled bleeding, loss of consciousness.

Off-peak hours (guarantee applies): Mon–Fri 9 AM–12 PM and 2 PM–5 PM.
Terms (keep on page): Applies to walk-in patients checking in during off-peak hours when the queue is short at check-in. Not available during peak hours or on weekends. Waiver applies to your copay only, not to insurance-billed services. Clinical urgency always comes first: patients with serious conditions are seen first.

3.7 Chronic Condition Care
Headline: When your condition flares up and your doctor can’t see you today.
Body: Many urgent cares send chronic-condition patients to the ER. We don’t. We provide same-day stabilization, give you a written action plan, contact your primary care provider within 24 hours, and book your follow-up before you leave. If you don’t have a primary care provider, we’ll give you a referral list.
We help with: High blood pressure readings without emergency symptoms · high blood sugar without diabetic emergency · moderate asthma or COPD flare-ups · gout and acute arthritis flares · cellulitis (including a first IV antibiotic dose) · complicated UTIs.
Then: A text from our Care Navigator two days later to see how you’re doing.
3.8 Ultrasound & imaging
Headline: More answers under one roof.
Body: Alongside digital X-ray, our credentialed providers use point-of-care ultrasound to look at what’s causing your symptoms in real time.
Used for: Suspected blood clots in the leg · flank pain and kidney stones · upper-right abdominal pain (gallbladder) · lumps, bumps and abscesses · tendon and joint problems · limited heart screening for shortness of breath or palpitations · early pregnancy concerns (screening only, not a diagnostic scan).
Ultrasound is performed when your provider decides it is clinically appropriate.

3.10 Family Visits
Headline: One visit. The whole family.
Body: Book a 45-minute family block for up to four people: a child’s physical, a parent’s sore throat, a grandparent’s blood pressure check and a refill, all in one trip. Each person is seen and documented individually.
Available: Mon–Fri, 9 AM–12 PM and 2 PM–5 PM.
How: Choose “Family Visit” online. Enter each person’s name, age and reason for visit. We confirm within 2 hours.
